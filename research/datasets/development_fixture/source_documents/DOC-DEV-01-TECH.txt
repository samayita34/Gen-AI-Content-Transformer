# Cloud Infrastructure SLA and Reliability Report

## 1. System Overview and Availability
The CoreSync distributed storage platform maintains a service level agreement (SLA) target of 99.95% monthly uptime. During the third quarter of 2025, CoreSync achieved an actual uptime of 99.98% across all three active availability zones in the us-east region. The primary cluster operates on 128 dedicated compute nodes, each provisioned with 64 GB of memory and dual 10 Gbps network interfaces.

## 2. Latency Metrics and Incident Response
The average 95th percentile read latency remained at 18 milliseconds throughout Q3 2025, while write latency peaked at 42 milliseconds during batch synchronization windows. The automated incident detection pipeline triggered 4 warning alerts during the quarter, with a mean time to recovery (MTTR) of 6.5 minutes. Maximum sustained throughput reached 85,000 operations per second on September 14, 2025.
