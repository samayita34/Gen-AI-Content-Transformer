# 1. Executive Summary
TransformAI is a research platform addressing automated multimodal content transformation.
It converts complex technical source documents into executive summaries, advisories, and presentation notes.

# 2. Dense Vector Indexing
The platform employs 384-dimensional dense sentence embeddings with pgvector indexing.
Cosine similarity is calculated as 1 minus cosine distance.
Chunk provenance preserves physical page numbers, section headings, and sentence boundaries.

# 3. Context Normalization
Normalized context provides downstream generative modules with factual units, key points, and entities.
Zero hallucinations or synthetic claim rewriting is allowed in the retrieval layer.
